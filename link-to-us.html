UK Rugby Club Directory

Link to the Directory

Copy-and-paste links and badges for clubs, councils, schools and rugby websites.

The UK Rugby Club Directory is a free listing of rugby union and rugby league clubs across the UK. If you run a club, a county body, a council or community website, a school or university, or a rugby blog, you are welcome to link to it. This page has ready-to-use links and badges.

Which page should I link to?

If you are…Link to…
A rugby clubYour own club page (find it via All Clubs) — not listed yet? Register free, or claim and update your listing.
A county or regional rugby body, or a local authorityThe area guide for your county or city (table below).
A school, college or universitySchool and university rugby, junior and youth clubs or women's clubs.
A blogger, journalist or forum moderatorUK rugby leagues, union league structure or club statistics.
Replacing a dead link to a club websiteThe club's page here, which links onward to its official website and England Rugby (or the national body) where we hold the details.

Click the box to select it, then copy and paste.

How is Rugby Joints compiled and checked?

  • Last reviewed: 26 November 2026
  • Maintained by: the UK Rugby Club Directory team, Horley, Surrey. See our About page and editorial standards.
  • Order: listings are alphabetical within each section, with featured listings first.

We add a business when it is rugby-specific or serves rugby clubs in the UK. Descriptions are written in our own words, and every listing has its own profile page that links to the organisation's own website so you can confirm current details.

Listing here is not an endorsement. We are not part of, or endorsed by, any business or governing body listed.

How do I correct or claim a listing?

Email hello@ukrugbyclubdirectory.co.uk or use the contact form, with a link to the listing and a source for the change. Business owners can also claim a listing or register a new rugby business. Plans are on the pricing page.

Looking for clubs instead? Browse rugby union clubs by region, rugby league clubs or the full area index.

What do people ask about Rugby Joints?

The Blogs, Podcasts & Fan Communities section lists 22 independent sites, including Scottish Rugby Blog, The Offside Line, EggChasers, Blood & Mud and The East Terrace. It also covers grassroots podcasts such as Roots Ruck Riot and Our Rugby Club Podcast. Each card links to the site itself.

League Express and Rugby League World Magazine are the long-running print titles, and NewsNow gathers league stories into one feed. Widnes Rugby is a supporter forum for Widnes Vikings fans.

Rugby Coach Weekly offers drills and session plans, and The Rugby Site publishes tactical video analysis. Coaching Rugby Ltd runs courses and camps. The governing bodies in the last section also list coaching and club-support pages.

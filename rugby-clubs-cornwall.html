Rugby is a big part of community life in Cornwall. This page lists the 16 Cornish clubs in our directory — 15 rugby union and 1 rugby league.

Cornish Pirates are the best-known senior club. Community clubs include Redruth, Camborne, Truro, Launceston, St Austell, Falmouth, Newquay Hornets, with Cornwall RLFC flying the flag for rugby league.

Rugby Clubs in Cornwall FAQs

How many rugby clubs are there in Cornwall?

Our directory currently lists 16 clubs in Cornwall — 15 rugby union and 1 rugby league. If a club is missing, you can register it for free.

Yes, Cornwall RLFC is listed in our directory, although most Cornish clubs play rugby union.

London has more rugby than anywhere else in the UK, from Premiership giants to Saturday-afternoon community sides. This page lists the 28 London clubs in our directory, grouped by area so you can find one you can actually get to on a weeknight.

At the top of the game, Harlequins play at the Twickenham Stoop and Saracens in north London, and both run women's sides too. Below them sit long-established clubs such as Blackheath and Rosslyn Park, and a large network of community clubs across every borough. The capital's rugby league is led by London Broncos, with London Skolars in the north of the city.

Travel matters more in London than anywhere else. Most players pick a club near home or work rather than the "best" club, so use the town list below to narrow things down first.

Quick Answers

Rugby Clubs in London FAQs

How many rugby clubs are there in London?

Our directory currently lists 28 clubs in London — 26 rugby union and 2 rugby league. If a club is missing, you can register it for free.

Are there rugby league clubs in London?

Yes. London Broncos are the capital's best-known rugby league club, and London Skolars are based in north London. Most London clubs, however, play rugby union.

Where are the professional rugby clubs in London?

Harlequins play at the Twickenham Stoop in south-west London and Saracens in north London. Both run community programmes alongside their professional teams.

How do I join a rugby club in London?

Pick two or three clubs near you, check each club's profile for where it plays and how to get in touch, then ask about the next training session. Our guide to finding a rugby club walks you through it.

Rugby Clubs in Surrey

Browse 14 Surrey rugby clubs, from historic Richmond to community sides in Guildford, Dorking and Farnham. Then compare grounds and get in touch.

Looking for Surrey rugby clubs? This page brings together all 14 rugby union clubs in Surrey that we list, so you can compare them side by side. Every club links to its own profile with its location, history and, where we hold them, contact details.

Surrey has deep rugby roots. For example, Richmond RFC dates from 1861, which places it among the oldest clubs in the game, while Dorking RFC has played since 1921. Meanwhile, towns such as Guildford, Farnham and Reigate each have their own long-established community club.

How to Choose a Rugby Club in Surrey

First, think about travel. Most players train twice a week and play on Saturdays, so a club within 20 minutes of home makes a real difference over a season.

Next, consider the level you want to play at. Some Surrey clubs compete in the National Leagues, while others focus on county and social rugby. Because most clubs run several senior teams, however, there is usually a side that suits your ability.

Finally, if you have children, ask about mini and junior sections. Our guide to junior and youth rugby explains the age groups and what to expect.

Rugby Clubs Around Guildford

Guildford RFC is the main club for Guildford and Godalming. It formed in 2003, when the adult sections of Guildford & Godalming RFC and Old Guildfordians merged, and is based at Broadwater, near Godalming.

Surrey Clubs in the Richmond Area

Richmond is now part of Greater London, yet it remains a historic home of Surrey rugby. Richmond RFC and London Welsh RFC appear on this page for that reason. In addition, you can explore every rugby union club in the UK.

Key takeaways

  • Rugby clubs in Surrey are all rugby union. Our directory lists 14, from Richmond in Champ Rugby to village clubs such as Cranleigh and Haslemere.
  • Surrey is strong at National League level: Esher, Dorking and London Welsh all play in National League 2 East.
  • Around Guildford there are two clubs: Guildford RFC (Regional 2 South Central) and Guildford & Godalming RFC.

Surrey rugby clubs by league

Surrey rugby clubs cover every level of the community game. Richmond play in Champ Rugby. Esher, Dorking and London Welsh are in National League 2 East, and Farnham in Regional 1 South Central. Guildford and Camberley are in Regional 2 South Central and Sutton & Epsom in Regional 2 South East, with village clubs in the Counties leagues.

Surrey rugby near the London and Sussex borders

North Surrey clubs such as Richmond, London Welsh and Esher are effectively part of the London rugby scene. Clubs in the south of the county, around Reigate, Cranleigh and Haslemere, are closer to Sussex. Surrey Rugby Clubs FAQs

How many rugby clubs are there in Surrey?

Our directory currently lists 14 rugby union clubs in Surrey. We add new clubs regularly, so check back or register a missing club.

What is the oldest rugby club in Surrey?

Of the clubs we list, Richmond RFC is the oldest, dating from 1861.

Which Surrey rugby clubs are in Guildford?

Guildford RFC is the town's club. It was formed in 2003 by a merger that included the adult section of Guildford & Godalming RFC.

Can adult beginners join a Surrey rugby club?

Yes. In fact, most clubs welcome adult beginners and run social or development teams. Contact the club first, then go along to a training session.

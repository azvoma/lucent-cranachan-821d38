Surrey Rugby Clubs

Looking for Surrey rugby clubs? This page brings together all 14 rugby union clubs in Surrey that we list, so you can compare them side by side. Every club links to its own profile with its location, history and, where we hold them, contact details.

Surrey has deep rugby roots. For example, Richmond RFC dates from 1861, which places it among the oldest clubs in the game, while Dorking RFC has played since 1921. Meanwhile, towns such as Guildford, Farnham and Reigate each have their own long-established community club.

How to Choose a Rugby Club in Surrey

First, think about travel. Most players train twice a week and play on Saturdays, so a club within 20 minutes of home makes a real difference over a season.

Next, consider the level you want to play at. Some Surrey clubs compete in the National Leagues, while others focus on county and social rugby. Because most clubs run several senior teams, however, there is usually a side that suits your ability.

Finally, if you have children, ask about mini and junior sections. Our guide to junior and youth rugby explains the age groups and what to expect.

Rugby Clubs Around Guildford

Guildford RFC is the main club for Guildford and Godalming. It formed in 2003, when the adult sections of Guildford & Godalming RFC and Old Guildfordians merged, and is based at Broadwater, near Godalming.

FAQs

The RFU registers over 1,900 rugby union clubs in England alone, with a further 800+ clubs across Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The UK Rugby Club Directory lists 470 of the most prominent clubs. Use the search tool above to find rugby union clubs near you.

Rugby union is played with 15 players per side under World Rugby rules. Rugby league is played with 13 players per side and is strongest in the North of England. Both codes are covered in this directory — use the filter buttons to search union or league clubs separately.

Almost all community rugby union clubs actively welcome adult beginners. Find a club near you using this directory, contact them via their profile page, and ask about beginner or social rugby sessions. Most clubs offer a free trial before committing to membership.

Below the Gallagher Premiership and RFU Championship, the English pyramid runs through National Leagues 1 and 2 and then regional divisions — London & SE, Midlands, North and South West — all the way to county and local league level.

Yes — most rugby union clubs now have dedicated women's and girls' sections. The RFU's Allianz Premier 15s is the top domestic women's league in England, with a full pyramid of regional and county leagues below it.

Merseyside is split between two rugby traditions. This page lists the 15 Merseyside clubs in our directory — 9 rugby league and 6 rugby union.

St Helens is a rugby league town through and through: alongside the professional St Helens club there are amateur sides such as Thatto Heath Crusaders and Pilkington Recs. Rugby union is centred on Liverpool, Southport, Crosby and the Wirral, with clubs such as Waterloo and Wirral.

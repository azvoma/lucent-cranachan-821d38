Rugby Clubs in the Scottish Borders

Compare 9 rugby clubs in the Scottish Borders, see where each one plays, and find the right club near you.

The Scottish Borders is one of the best-known rugby areas in Britain. This page lists the 9 Borders clubs in our directory.

Clubs include Hawick, Melrose, Gala, Kelso, Selkirk, Jed-Forest, Jedburgh, Coldstream, Eyemouth. Melrose is also the home of the sevens game, first played there in 1883.

For the capital, see rugby clubs in Edinburgh, or browse all Scottish clubs.

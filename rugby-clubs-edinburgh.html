Edinburgh is the home of Scottish rugby, with Murrayfield on its doorstep and some of the oldest clubs in the world. This page lists the 10 clubs in our directory in Edinburgh and the Lothians.

At the top, Edinburgh Rugby are the city's professional side. Below them are historic clubs such as Watsonians, Heriot's and Boroughmuir, and community clubs across East and West Lothian.

Key takeaways

  • Rugby clubs in Edinburgh are all rugby union. Edinburgh Rugby play in the United Rugby Championship, and the city has some of Scotland's oldest club sides.
  • Club rugby in the city is led by Watsonians (founded 1875), Heriot's, Boroughmuir and Currie Chieftains.
  • East and West Lothian add Musselburgh, Haddington, Linlithgow and Livingston.

Edinburgh rugby teams

Edinburgh's rugby teams range from the professional Edinburgh Rugby side, who play in the United Rugby Championship, to long-established clubs. Watsonians, Heriot's, Boroughmuir, Inverleith and Currie Chieftains all run adult, women's or junior teams. Most club sides play in the Scottish Premiership or the National Leagues.

Joining a rugby club in Edinburgh

To join a rugby club in Edinburgh, choose by area and level. The big city clubs field several adult teams, so beginners and experienced players both get games. Lothian clubs such as Musselburgh, Haddington and Linlithgow are community-focused. Scottish clubs need coaches working with children to be PVG-checked, so ask about this when joining a junior section.

Scottish club rugby has its own league system: the Scottish Premiership, then National Leagues One to Four, then regional leagues. See our guide to rugby leagues in Scotland.

Rugby teams in Edinburgh: professional and club rugby

Rugby teams in Edinburgh range from Edinburgh Rugby, the city's professional side in the United Rugby Championship, to amateur clubs in the Scottish leagues. Many Edinburgh rugby clubs began as former pupils' clubs of city schools, and most now welcome any player.

  • Professional: Edinburgh Rugby (URC).
  • City clubs: Watsonians, Heriot's, Boroughmuir and Inverleith.
  • Lothians: Currie Chieftains, Musselburgh, Haddington, Linlithgow and Livingston.

Scottish Rugby has its headquarters at Murrayfield in the west of the city. A rugby club in Edinburgh is affiliated to Scottish Rugby, not the RFU, so use Scottish Rugby to check leagues and age-grade sections.

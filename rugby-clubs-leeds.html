Leeds rugby union and rugby league clubs sit side by side across the city. This page lists all 11 Leeds clubs in our directory, so you can compare both codes in one place. Then open any profile for the ground and contact details.

Leeds Rugby Union Clubs

If you want to play Leeds rugby union, start with Headingley RFC, which dates from 1878. In addition, Roundhegians RFC and Bramley Phoenix RUFC offer 15-a-side rugby in the suburbs, with a welcoming community feel.

Rugby League in Leeds

Leeds Rhinos are the city's professional rugby league club. Meanwhile, Hunslet RLFC flies the flag for South Leeds, and amateur clubs such as East Leeds ARLFC and Stanningley ARLFC keep the community game thriving.

Leeds Rugby Clubs FAQs

Where can I play Leeds rugby union?

Headingley RFC, Roundhegians RFC and Bramley Phoenix RUFC all offer rugby union in Leeds. So you can choose the club closest to home.

Which professional rugby club plays in Leeds?

Leeds Rhinos are the city's professional rugby league club.

How many rugby clubs are in Leeds?

We currently list 11 Leeds clubs: 3 rugby union and 8 rugby league. More Leeds clubs are added regularly.

Rugby union is played with 15 players per side under World Rugby rules. Rugby league is played with 13 players per side and is strongest in the North of England. Both codes are covered in this directory — use the filter buttons to search union or league clubs separately.

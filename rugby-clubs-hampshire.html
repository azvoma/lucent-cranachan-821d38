Hampshire has rugby union clubs from the New Forest to the Surrey border. This page lists the 9 Hampshire clubs in our directory so you can compare them in one place.

Around Southampton, Trojans and Eastleigh are based in Eastleigh, with Tottonians in Totton. Further north and east there are clubs in Winchester, Basingstoke, Andover, Alton, Petersfield and Havant.

Clubs near the Surrey border are also within reach of our Surrey rugby clubs.

Looking for Surrey rugby clubs? This page brings together all 14 rugby union clubs in Surrey that we list, so you can compare them side by side. In addition, every club links to its own profile with the ground, history and contact details.

Surrey has deep rugby roots. For example, Richmond RFC dates from 1861 and Dorking RFC from 1871, which places both among the oldest clubs in the game. Meanwhile, towns such as Guildford, Farnham and Reigate each have their own long-established community club.

How to Choose a Rugby Club in Surrey

First, think about travel. Most players train twice a week and play on Saturdays, so a club within 20 minutes of home makes a real difference over a season.

Next, consider the level you want to play at. Some Surrey clubs compete in the National Leagues, while others focus on county and social rugby. Because most clubs run several senior teams, however, there is usually a side that suits your ability.

Finally, if you have children, ask about mini and junior sections. Our guide to junior and youth rugby explains the age groups and what to expect.

Rugby Clubs Around Guildford

Guildford has two senior clubs. Guildford RFC is the county town's own club, while Guildford & Godalming RFC serves Guildford and neighbouring Godalming. So if you live in the area, it is worth visiting both before you decide.

Surrey Clubs in the Richmond Area

Richmond is now part of Greater London, yet it remains a historic home of Surrey rugby. Richmond RFC, London Welsh RFC and London Cornish RFC all appear on this page for that reason. In addition, you can explore every rugby union club in the UK.

Surrey Rugby Clubs FAQs

How many rugby clubs are there in Surrey?

Our directory currently lists 14 rugby union clubs in Surrey. We add new clubs regularly, so check back or register a missing club.

What is the oldest rugby club in Surrey?

Of the clubs we list, Richmond RFC is the oldest, dating from 1861. Dorking RFC follows closely, with a founding date of 1871.

Which Surrey rugby clubs are in Guildford?

Guildford has Guildford RFC and Guildford & Godalming RFC. Both run senior rugby, so contact each club to compare training nights and teams.

Can adult beginners join a Surrey rugby club?

Yes. In fact, most clubs welcome adult beginners and run social or development teams. Contact the club first, then go along to a training session.

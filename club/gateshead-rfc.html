About the Club

About Gateshead RFC

Gateshead RFC is a community rugby union club at Hedley Lawson Park in Low Fell, Gateshead. The club was formed in 1998 by the merger of two long-standing clubs, North Durham RFC and Gateshead Fell RFC.

The clubhouse, known locally as The Fell, is a popular local venue and is listed by CAMRA for its real ale.

Our Story

History of Gateshead RFC

In 1921 it was proposed that the club be renamed Gateshead Fell Rugby Football Club, and its ground was officially opened as Hedley Lawson Park in 1938 in memory of Hedley Lawson, who had done much work for the club. North Durham RFC, founded in 1876, played at the North Durham cricket ground. The two merged in 1998 to form Gateshead RFC.

About the Club

About Consett RFC

Consett RFC is a rugby union club in Consett, County Durham, founded in 1926. It is a County Durham steel town club competing in North East leagues with a strong working-class tradition.

As one of 13 rugby union clubs we list in County Durham, Consett RFC gives local players a place to train, compete and belong. Whether you are new to the sport or an experienced player, this page shows you where the club plays and how to get involved.

Our Story

History of Consett RFC

Consett RFC formed in 1926, in the years between the two World Wars. Since then, the club has carried amateur rugby through almost a century of change.

Today, the club carries more than 100 years of history into every season. For players in Consett, County Durham, that heritage adds real meaning to pulling on the shirt.

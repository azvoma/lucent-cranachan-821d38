About the Club

About Oldham RLFC

Oldham RLFC is a rugby league club in Oldham, Greater Manchester, founded in 1876. It is a Greater Manchester club competing in the Championship, with a proud history of producing international players.

The club is one of 15 rugby league clubs we list in Greater Manchester. In addition, it gives supporters in Oldham a team to follow and new players a clear route into the game.

Our Story

History of Oldham RLFC

Oldham RLFC dates from 1876, before the 1895 split that created the Northern Union. That breakaway, agreed in Huddersfield, became the game we now call rugby league.

Today, the club carries more than 150 years of history into every season. For players in Oldham, Greater Manchester, that heritage adds real meaning to pulling on the shirt.

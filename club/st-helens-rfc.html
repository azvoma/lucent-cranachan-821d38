About the Club

About St Helens Rugby Club (Saints)

St Helens R.F.C., known as Saints, is a professional rugby league club in St Helens, Merseyside, founded in 1873. They play in Super League at the BrewDog Stadium, formerly the Totally Wicked Stadium, in the Peasley Cross area of the town.

Despite "RFC" in the name, this is not a rugby union club. St Helens RFC (Union) is listed separately.

Our Story

History of St Helens RFC

St Helens was founded in 1873 and was a founder member of the Northern Union in 1895. The club left Knowsley Road in 2011 for a new stadium, which opened in 2012 as Langtree Park. The ground was called the Totally Wicked Stadium from 2017 to 2025. In October 2025 the club agreed a ten-year partnership with BrewDog, and the home of the Saints became the BrewDog Stadium.

Is there a St Helens rugby union club?

Yes. St Helens RFC (Union) is the town's rugby union club and is listed separately from the Saints. People searching for St Helens rugby union are usually looking for that club or for others on Merseyside, which our Merseyside guide covers.

Quick Answers

St Helens Rugby Club FAQs: address, stadium and league

Where do St Helens rugby league club play?

St Helens play at the BrewDog Stadium, McManus Drive, St Helens WA9 3AL. The ground opened in 2012 as Langtree Park and was the Totally Wicked Stadium from 2017 to 2025.

When was St Helens RFC founded?

The Saints were founded in 1873 and were one of the founder clubs of the Northern Union in 1895.

Is St Helens RFC a rugby union or rugby league club?

This profile is for the Saints, a rugby league club. St Helens RFC (Union) is a separate rugby union club in the town.

Can I park near the stadium?

Do not park at the neighbouring retail park or Tesco Extra unless you are visiting those stores, because you may be charged a fixed penalty. Check the club's match-day guide for official parking.

What league does St Helens RFC play in?

In 2026 St Helens RFC plays in Super League, the top tier of rugby league. See how the rugby league competitions work.

Where does St Helens RFC play?

St Helens play at the BrewDog Stadium, McManus Drive, Peasley Cross, St Helens WA9 3AL. It was called the Totally Wicked Stadium until 2025 and Langtree Park before that.

When was St Helens RFC founded?

St Helens RFC was founded in 1873.

How can I join St Helens RFC?

St Helens RFC is a professional Super League club, so it does not run open-age teams for the public. To play rugby league yourself, join an amateur club near you; see our list of rugby clubs in Merseyside.

Which other rugby league clubs are near St Helens?

Nearby clubs in our directory include Thatto Heath Crusaders, Pilkington Recs ARLFC and Clock Face Miners ARLFC. Each has its own profile below.

What is the St Helens rugby club address?

The St Helens rugby club address is the BrewDog Stadium, McManus Drive, Peasley Cross, St Helens WA9 3AL. Use the postcode WA9 3AL for sat-nav and directions; the stadium is beside the A58 Linkway and about 20 minutes on foot from St Helens Central station.

Is St Helens RFC the same as St Helens RLFC?

Yes. The club's formal name is St Helens RFC (Rugby Football Club), but it plays rugby league, so it is often written St Helens RLFC or St Helens RFLC. For rugby union in the town, see St Helens RFC (Union) and rugby clubs in Merseyside.

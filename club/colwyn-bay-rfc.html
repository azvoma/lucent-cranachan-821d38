About the Club

About Colwyn Bay RFC

Colwyn Bay RFC is a rugby union club in Colwyn Bay, Conwy, founded in 1896. It is a North Wales seaside resort club competing in North Wales leagues.

The club is one of three rugby union clubs we list in Conwy. In addition, it gives supporters in Colwyn Bay a team to follow and new players a clear route into the game.

Our Story

History of Colwyn Bay RFC

Colwyn Bay RFC formed in 1896, in the years before the First World War. Since then, like many clubs of that era, it has seen the game change almost beyond recognition.

In total, the club has more than 130 years behind it. As a result, it holds a special place for supporters across Conwy.

About the Club

About Abercarn RFC

Abercarn RFC is a Welsh rugby union club in Abercarn, Caerphilly, founded in 1895. The club plays at Abercarn Welfare Ground and is a feeder club for the Dragons.

In 1950 the club lost three members, including its captain and coach, in the Llandow air disaster. The club badge carries a plane propeller in their memory.

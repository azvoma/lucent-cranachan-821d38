About Widnes Rugby

Widnes Rugby is a Widnes Vikings supporter community. Independent supporter community and forum for Widnes Vikings, with news, match commentary and club history.

Best for Vikings fans who want to talk rugby league. Rugby Joints lists it in the Blogs, Podcasts & Fan Communities section.

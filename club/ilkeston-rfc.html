About the Club

About Ilkeston RFC

East Derbyshire/West Nottinghamshire club competing in Midlands leagues with a welcoming ethos.

Ilkeston RFC has been a proud part of the rugby union community in Ilkeston, Derbyshire since 1930. The club welcomes players of all ages and abilities and is committed to developing rugby from grassroots to senior level.

Our Story

History & Heritage

Ilkeston RFC was founded in 1930, giving the club over 95 years of history in Ilkeston, Derbyshire. Over the decades the club has grown from humble beginnings into a thriving community organisation.

If you are looking for a rugby union club in Ilkeston, Ilkeston RFC could be the perfect fit. With a welcoming community, dedicated coaching staff and teams at multiple levels, there is something for every player.

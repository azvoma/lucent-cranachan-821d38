Hull Rugby Teams

7 Hull rugby teams in one place: two Super League giants, community rugby league and rugby union. Compare them all below.

Hull rugby teams are among the most famous in the sport. This page lists all 7 clubs in the city that we hold, from professional rugby league to amateur and rugby union sides. In addition, each club links to its own full profile.

The city is split by the River Hull. Traditionally, Hull FC represents the west of the city, while Hull Kingston Rovers represents the east. As a result, the Hull derby is one of the fiercest rivalries in rugby league.

Rugby League in Hull

Beyond the two professional clubs, Hull has a strong amateur game. For example, West Hull ARLFC, East Hull ARLFC, Hull Dockers ARLFC and Myton Warriors ARLFC all develop players in the community.

Rugby Union in Hull

Rugby league dominates the city, yet union also has a home here. Hull RUFC offers 15-a-side rugby, so players who prefer union can still play close to home.

Quick Answers

Hull Rugby Teams FAQs

What are the professional Hull rugby teams?

Hull has two professional rugby league clubs: Hull FC and Hull Kingston Rovers. Both are among the most famous Hull rugby teams.

Is there a rugby union club in Hull?

Yes, there is. Hull RUFC plays rugby union in the city.

Can I play amateur rugby league in Hull?

Yes, you can. In fact, clubs such as West Hull ARLFC and East Hull ARLFC welcome players of all ages and abilities.

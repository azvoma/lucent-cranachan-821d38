About Kippax Welfare ARLFC

Kippax Welfare ARLFC is a rugby league club in Kippax, West Yorkshire, founded in 1929. It is a West Yorkshire village club competing in the National Conference League.

Kippax Welfare ARLFC is one of 36 rugby league clubs we list in West Yorkshire. Because every club has its own character, this profile helps you see what makes this one different before you visit.

History of Kippax Welfare ARLFC

Kippax Welfare ARLFC formed in 1929, and it has grown alongside the modern 13-a-side game. For decades, clubs like this one have kept rugby league at the heart of their communities.

About the Business

About Irish Rugby Football Union

The Irish Rugby Football Union (IRFU) is the governing body for rugby union across the island of Ireland, in both the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland. It is based in Dublin and was founded in 1879.

This is a governing-body listing, not a playing club. To find an Irish club, use the Energia All-Ireland League and Northern Ireland club information below.

The IRFU was formed in 1879 and organises the game through four provincial branches: Leinster, Munster, Ulster and Connacht. Ulster covers Northern Ireland. The IRFU runs the Energia All-Ireland League for men and women, the top level of club rugby on the island.

What They Offer

Irish Rugby Football Union Services

Irish club rugby sits below the provinces. The top amateur clubs play in the Energia All-Ireland League, and the other Irish rugby clubs play in provincial leagues run by each branch. Clubs in Northern Ireland come under the Ulster Branch. For rugby union in Ireland at club level, start with the IRFU’s official website or see rugby leagues in the UK.

The UK Rugby Club Directory lists the IRFU alongside the other governing bodies of the home nations: the RFU, WRU, Scottish Rugby and RFL.

How does Irish club rugby work?

Irish club rugby is organised through the IRFU and its four provincial branches. The Energia All-Ireland League sits at the top, with five men's divisions of 10 teams in 2025-26. Below it, provincial leagues in Leinster, Munster, Ulster and Connacht feed promotion into the AIL.

Level Competition Notes
Professional United Rugby Championship Leinster, Munster, Ulster and Connacht
Top club level Energia All-Ireland League (AIL) Five men's divisions in 2025-26: 1A, 1B, 2A, 2B and 2C
Provincial leagues Leinster, Munster, Ulster and Connacht leagues Winners play off for AIL places
Community Local club leagues and junior rugby Run by each province

2025-26: St Mary's College won their third AIL title, with Clontarf runners-up.

2026-27: the IRFU is merging Divisions 2B and 2C and splitting them geographically into 2BN (North) and 2BS (South). Clubs placed in the new divisions include Banbridge, Malone, Rainey Old Boys, Ballyclare, Bective Rangers, Clonmel, Enniscorthy and Skerries.

The women's AIL is also being restructured for 2026-27, so check irishrugby.ie for the current format.

What Irish rugby clubs are in Northern Ireland?

Our directory lists Armagh, Ballymena, Dungannon, Instonians and Malone, plus Ulster Rugby, for Northern Ireland. They fall under the IRFU's Ulster branch, and several play in the Energia All-Ireland League, so they are the best starting point for club rugby in the UK part of Ireland.

Quick Answers

Irish Rugby Football Union FAQs

What is the IRFU?

The IRFU is the Irish Rugby Football Union, the governing body for rugby union across the whole island of Ireland. It was founded in 1879 and is based in Dublin.

What is the Energia All-Ireland League?

It is the top level of club rugby in Ireland, run by the IRFU. In 2025-26 it had five men's divisions of 10 teams, and St Mary's College won the title.

Does the IRFU cover Northern Ireland?

Yes. Rugby union in Northern Ireland is run through the IRFU's Ulster branch, and Northern Ireland clubs compete in the same league system as clubs in the Republic.

How do I find an Irish rugby club to join?

Start with the IRFU's club information or the Ulster Rugby site for clubs in Northern Ireland. Our directory also lists Northern Ireland clubs with their towns.

Where is Irish Rugby Football Union based?

The IRFU is based on Lansdowne Road in Dublin, the home of the Aviva Stadium. Use the map link on this page for directions.

What does Irish Rugby Football Union offer?

It is the governing body for rugby union across the island of Ireland, running the national teams, the four provinces and Irish club rugby, including the All-Ireland League.

How do I contact Irish Rugby Football Union?

Visit the IRFU's official website, irishrugby.ie, using the link above. For a club near you, contact the provincial branch.

How many rugby clubs are there in Ireland?

There are about 200 rugby clubs in Ireland affiliated to the IRFU across its four branches. Leinster has the most and Connacht the fewest. Fifty of them play in the five divisions of the All-Ireland League.

What are the Irish rugby union teams?

At the top are the Ireland national teams and the four professional provinces, Connacht, Leinster, Munster and Ulster, which play in the United Rugby Championship. Below them, Irish rugby clubs play in the All-Ireland League and provincial leagues.

How is IRFU club rugby organised?

IRFU club rugby has the Energia All-Ireland League at the top, with five divisions. Below that, each provincial branch runs its own leagues and cups. Clubs in Northern Ireland are part of the Ulster Branch.

Who runs Irish club rugby and rugby union in Ireland?

The IRFU runs rugby union in Ireland, from the national team to Irish club rugby. Day-to-day club competitions are organised by the four provincial branches, and the All-Ireland League is the top club competition.

Is it the Ireland Rugby Football Union or the Irish Rugby Football Union?

The official name is the Irish Rugby Football Union (IRFU), though people often search for the Ireland Rugby Football Union. It covers Irish rugby clubs north and south of the border.

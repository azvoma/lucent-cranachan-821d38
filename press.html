About the directory

The UK Rugby Club Directory is a free online directory of rugby union and rugby league clubs across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. It currently holds 570 club profiles (469 union, 101 league), each with its own page, plus area guides, league guides and advice for people looking for a club to join.

Useful pages

  • Rugby club statistics — clubs by nation, code and county
  • UK rugby leagues guide and union league structure
  • Area guides for counties and cities
  • Women's rugby clubs, junior and youth rugby and school and university rugby
  • How to find a rugby club near you and how to start playing rugby as an adult
  • Editorial standards — where our information comes from and how it is checked

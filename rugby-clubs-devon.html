Devon has a rugby union club in almost every town. This page lists the 21 Devon clubs in our directory so you can compare them in one place.

At the top are Exeter Chiefs (with Exeter Chiefs Women) and Plymouth Albion. Community clubs include Exeter, Barnstaple, Bideford, Torquay Athletic, Newton Abbot, Tavistock, Exmouth.

See also the wider South West rugby clubs page, and rugby clubs in Cornwall next door.

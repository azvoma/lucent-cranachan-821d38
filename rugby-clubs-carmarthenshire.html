Carmarthenshire is a West Wales rugby heartland. This page lists the 9 clubs in our directory across Llanelli, Carmarthen, Llandovery and Ammanford.

Scarlets are the region's professional side, with community clubs such as Llanelli, Carmarthen Quins, Llandovery, Amman United, Tycroes, Tumble, Brynamman, and West Wales Raiders for rugby league.

For the capital see Cardiff rugby clubs, or browse all Welsh clubs.

Away fan & matchday guide

  • Pitch surface3G artificial
  • Parking & overflowFree on-site car park (approx. 60 spaces) with overflow on the lower field on busy days.
  • Clubhouse bar & foodClubhouse bar open from 12:30 on match days. Post-match hot food, tea and coffee served from the kitchen hatch.
  • Spectator comfortCovered stand for around 150, floodlights on the main pitch, step-free access from the car park.
  • Dog policyDogs welcome on leads

Visiting teams change in the main clubhouse with hot showers. Please check in with the fixtures secretary on arrival. The ground is a short signposted walk from the village centre, and the bus stop on Church Lane is two minutes away. Home fixtures usually kick off at 14:30 unless stated; the club posts any weather or pitch inspection updates on Facebook by 10:00 on match days.

Club heritage, honours & alumni

Brackenfield Vale Rugby Football Club was founded in 1921 by a group of colliery clerks, farm workers and returning servicemen who wanted a winter game for the village. Early fixtures were played on a rented field behind the Fox and Hounds, with the changing rooms little more than a hired marquee. The club moved to its present home at Vale Park in 1958 after a local landowner agreed to lease the ground for a peppercorn rent, and volunteers built the first clubhouse over three summers. The black and amber hoops have been worn without a break since the 1930s.

Through the 1970s and 1980s the club built a strong reputation for its colts section, sending a steady stream of players to county and divisional sides. A women's team was established in 2012 and now fields a full XV each season, and the minis section has grown to more than 150 registered children. Floodlights were installed in 2004 and the 3G training pitch opened in 2022, funded by a community appeal and a sports foundation grant.

Today Brackenfield Vale is run entirely by volunteers and remains the social centre of the village on Saturdays.

England has some of the finest grassroots rugby in the world. With hundreds of amateur clubs spread across every county, the question isn't whether you can find rugby near you — it's which county has the very best rugby culture.

## Yorkshire

Yorkshire is the spiritual home of both rugby codes in England. With Rugby League powerhouses like Leeds Rhinos and Castleford Tigers sitting alongside a thriving union club scene, the county offers more rugby per square mile than anywhere else in the country. Clubs like Halifax RUFC, Roundhay RFC, and Bradford Salem have storied amateur histories stretching back well over a century.

## The South West

Cornwall, Devon, Somerset, and Gloucestershire form one of England's most passionate rugby regions. The South West has a fierce county championship rivalry and clubs like Exeter RFC, Clifton RFC, and Redruth RFC are deeply embedded in their communities. The region consistently produces England internationals at all age groups.

## The Midlands

The Midlands punches above its weight in rugby union. Warwickshire, Leicestershire, and Staffordshire all have dense club networks. The presence of Leicester Tigers has elevated the entire region's rugby culture — and their community outreach means youth rugby participation in the East Midlands is among the highest in England.

## London and the South East

London's rugby scene is enormous but often overlooked. Clubs like Richmond RFC, Rosslyn Park, and London Welsh have histories as rich as any in England. Surrey and Kent both have strong county structures and excellent junior sections.

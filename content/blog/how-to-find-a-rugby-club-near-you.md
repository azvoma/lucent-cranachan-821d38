Whether you've never picked up a rugby ball before or you're relocating to a new part of the country, finding the right rugby club can feel daunting. This guide walks you through everything you need to know.

## Why Join a Rugby Club?

Rugby clubs aren't just about the sport. They're community hubs — social clubs, fitness centres, and friendship networks rolled into one. Most clubs across the UK welcome complete beginners at any age, and many run dedicated beginner sessions so you don't need to worry about experience.

## Step 1: Know Which Code You Want

There are two main codes of rugby in the UK:

- **Rugby Union** — 15-a-side, the most widely played form. Governed by England Rugby, Scottish Rugby, the WRU and Ulster Rugby.
- **Rugby League** — 13-a-side, predominant in Yorkshire and the North West of England.

If you're unsure, Rugby Union clubs are more geographically widespread across all four nations.

## Step 2: Search by Location

Use the search bar at the top of this site to find clubs in your town or county. You can filter by Rugby Union, Rugby League, or look for clubs specifically welcoming beginners.

## Step 3: Contact the Club Directly

Every club in our directory has its own profile page with contact details. Most clubs have a dedicated Membership Secretary or Welfare Officer — drop them an email and ask about trial sessions. Almost every club runs free come-and-try events throughout the season.

## Step 4: Turn Up and Play

The best thing about grassroots rugby in the UK is that the culture is genuinely welcoming. Bring your boots, a gumshield, and a willingness to learn. The rest will follow.

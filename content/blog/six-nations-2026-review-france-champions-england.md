England went into the 2026 Six Nations on the back of an unbeaten autumn, and plenty of supporters — us included — thought this could be the year they won a first championship since 2020. It wasn't. France won the title on the final weekend, and England finished fifth with a single win.

This review replaces the preview we published before the tournament, which no longer reflected what happened.

## The final table

France finished top on 21 points after beating England 48-46 at the Stade de France, with a late Thomas Ramos penalty deciding both the match and the championship. Ireland were second on 19 points and Scotland third on 16. Italy finished fourth, England fifth on 8 points and Wales sixth.

## England's results, round by round

1. **England 48-7 Wales** (Twickenham) — a strong opening that seemed to confirm the autumn form.
2. **Scotland 31-20 England** (Murrayfield) — the first defeat, and the start of a difficult run.
3. **England 21-42 Ireland** (Twickenham) — a heavy home loss.
4. **Italy 23-18 England** (Stadio Olimpico, Rome) — a narrow defeat in Rome.
5. **France 48-46 England** (Stade de France) — a remarkable final match, lost at the very end.

One win from five is a long way short of what the autumn results promised. The final game showed the attacking threat is real, but conceding 48 points — and 42 at home to Ireland — points to a defence that did not hold up across the championship.

## What it means for grassroots rugby

International results move numbers at community clubs. A good Six Nations tends to bring a wave of new juniors and returning adults in the spring; a disappointing one does not stop people wanting to play. If watching the championship made you want to pull your boots back on — or try rugby for the first time — the season at most community clubs runs from September to April, and new players are usually welcome at any point.

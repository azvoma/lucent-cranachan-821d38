Warwickshire is where the game of rugby is traditionally said to have started, at Rugby School in the town of Rugby. This page lists the 8 clubs in our directory across Warwickshire and Coventry.

Coventry are the leading club, with Broadstreet, Kenilworth, Leamington, Old Leamingtonians and Stratford-upon-Avon across the county.

Key takeaways

  • Rugby clubs in Warwickshire include the town of Rugby, where the game is traditionally said to have begun, and Rugby Football Club itself.
  • Coventry are the county's leading club, in Champ Rugby. Broadstreet play in Regional 1 Midlands and Kenilworth in Regional 2 Midlands West.
  • For Leamington Spa rugby, there are two clubs: Leamington RFC and Old Leamingtonians, both in Counties 1 Midlands West (South).

Warwickshire rugby clubs by league

Warwickshire rugby runs from Coventry in Champ Rugby to Broadstreet in Regional 1 Midlands and Kenilworth in Regional 2 Midlands West. Leamington and Old Leamingtonians play in Counties 1 Midlands West (South). Stratford-upon-Avon and Rugby have historic town clubs. For rugby league, Midlands Hurricanes are based in Coventry.

Rugby in Leamington Spa

Leamington Spa has two rugby union clubs, Leamington RFC (founded 1875) and Old Leamingtonians RFC (founded 1927). Both play in Counties 1 Midlands West (South), and both run senior and junior teams. Kenilworth, in Regional 2 Midlands West, is a ten-minute drive away for players wanting a higher level.

Old Leamingtonians are often shortened to "OLRFC" or "Old Leams". Search either name in the directory to find them. For clubs across the county boundary, see rugby clubs in Birmingham.

Warwickshire rugby clubs and the county union

Warwickshire rugby clubs play in RFU leagues and are supported locally by the Warwickshire Rugby Football Union, one of the RFU’s county bodies. It runs county cups and representative teams. The clubs on this page are in Coventry, Leamington Spa, Kenilworth, Rugby and Stratford-upon-Avon, so most players have a club within a short drive.

This page lists the 19 clubs in our directory across the West Midlands county — Birmingham, Coventry, Solihull, Wolverhampton and the Black Country.

Clubs include Coventry, Moseley, Birmingham & Solihull, Broadstreet, Sutton Coldfield, Stourbridge, Wolverhampton. Midlands Hurricanes play rugby league.

For the city alone see Birmingham rugby clubs, and for the surrounding county Warwickshire.

Somerset rugby clubs

Somerset rugby clubs include one of England's most famous professional clubs, Bath, founded in 1865, and a ring of community clubs in Taunton, Bridgwater, Weston-super-Mare, Clevedon, Nailsea and Yeovil. Taunton play in National League 2 West. Yeovil play in Counties 2 Dorset & Wilts South because of where the town sits.

Rugby near Bath and north Somerset

For rugby near Bath, Bath Rugby's community programmes and the clubs in Bristol and north Somerset are the closest options. Clevedon and Nailsea & Backwell sit between Bath, Bristol and Weston. Players in east Somerset may also find Wiltshire clubs such as Trowbridge, in Regional 1 South West, within reach.

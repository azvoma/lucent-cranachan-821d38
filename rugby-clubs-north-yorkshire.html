North Yorkshire is a big county with rugby in most of its towns. This page lists the 14 North Yorkshire clubs in our directory — 10 rugby union and 4 rugby league.

York is the only place in the county with both codes at a high level: York Knights play rugby league, alongside amateur league clubs and York RUFC. Elsewhere rugby union dominates, with clubs in Harrogate, Scarborough, Malton & Norton, Whitby, Skipton, Selby and on Teesside.

For West and South Yorkshire, see our Leeds and Hull pages or all Yorkshire rugby.

Rugby Clubs in North Yorkshire FAQs

How many rugby clubs are there in North Yorkshire?

Our directory currently lists 14 clubs in North Yorkshire — 10 rugby union and 4 rugby league. If a club is missing, you can register it for free.

Is there rugby league in York?

Yes. York Knights are the city's professional rugby league club, and there are amateur league clubs such as Heworth and New Earswick All Blacks.

How do I join a rugby club in North Yorkshire?

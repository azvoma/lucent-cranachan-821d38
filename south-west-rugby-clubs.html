South West Rugby Clubs

70 clubs across Devon, Cornwall, Somerset, Gloucestershire, Bristol, Dorset and Wiltshire, from the Premiership to the grassroots. This is South West rugby in one place.

South West rugby is some of the most passionate in England. This page lists all 70 clubs we hold across the region, grouped by county. So whether you live in Penzance or Swindon, you can find your nearest club in seconds.

At the top level, England's South West is home to four Premiership clubs: Bath Rugby, Bristol Bears, Exeter Chiefs and Gloucester Rugby. Below them, however, sits a deep network of community clubs, many of them well over a century old.

Why South West Rugby Stands Out

For many fans, Cornwall is the heartland of South West rugby. It treats rugby almost as a national sport. In fact, clubs such as Redruth RFC and Camborne RFC draw some of the most loyal crowds in the community game.

Beyond Cornwall, South West rugby has plenty of variety. Devon, Somerset and Gloucestershire, meanwhile, combine big city clubs with market-town sides. As a result, players in the South West can find everything from elite academies to relaxed social rugby within a short drive.

Quick Answers

South West Rugby FAQs

Which Premiership rugby clubs are in the South West?

Bath Rugby, Bristol Bears, Exeter Chiefs and Gloucester Rugby all play in the South West. Together, they make the region a powerhouse of the Premiership.

How many South West rugby clubs are in the directory?

We currently list 70 clubs across Devon, Cornwall, Somerset, Gloucestershire, Bristol, Dorset and Wiltshire. So South West rugby is one of the best-covered regions in the directory.

Is there rugby league in the South West?

Yes, there is. Cornwall RLFC brings professional rugby league to the region, although rugby union remains the main code.

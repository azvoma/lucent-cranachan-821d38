About Us & Editorial Standards

Where our club information comes from, how we check it, and how to tell us when something is wrong.

The UK Rugby Club Directory is a free, independent directory of rugby union and rugby league clubs across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. We are run by a small team based in Horley, Surrey. We are not part of, or endorsed by, any club or governing body.

Our aim is simple: help people find a rugby club they can join, and give them accurate information to get in touch.

Where our information comes from

Club listings are compiled from public sources, including clubs' own websites and social media pages, governing body websites, local council and community directories, and published references such as Wikipedia. Clubs can also add and correct their own details by claiming their listing.

How we check it

  • When we add contact details to a club's profile, we show the source we took them from and the date we checked it.
  • Where we don't hold contact details for a club, the profile says so rather than guessing, and points you to the right governing body.
  • We check founding dates and other facts against published sources where we can, and correct them when we find errors.
  • Details supplied by a club official through a claimed listing take priority over other sources.

Clubs change grounds, contacts and training nights, so some information will go out of date. If something looks wrong, please tell us.

Report an error

If you spot a mistake — a wrong founding date, an old phone number, a club that has merged or closed — email contact@ukrugbyclubdirectory.co.uk or use our contact form, with a link to the page and, if possible, a source. We review every correction and update the page once the change is confirmed.

Club badges and names

Club names and badges belong to the clubs. We show them only to identify each club. Where we don't have a club's badge, we show its initial instead. Club officials can ask us to add, change or remove a badge at any time.

Articles and guides

Our blog and guides explain how to find, join and start rugby clubs. When an article reports facts — results, dates, figures — we link to or name the source, and we update or replace articles that go out of date.

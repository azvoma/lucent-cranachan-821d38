Month 2–4 · Outreach

Club & Association Outreach

Email every club listed in the directory asking them to link back from their own website. Template below:

Subject: Your club is listed on UK Rugby Club Directory — free profile

Hi [Club Name] Team,

We've created a free profile page for [Club Name] on UK Rugby Club Directory at:
https://ukrugbyclubdirectory.co.uk/club/[slug]

It includes your contact details, location map and club description — completely free.

We'd love it if you could add a link from your website, e.g. "Find us on UK Rugby Club Directory".

You can also claim your listing to add photos, update details and manage your page.

Kind regards,
UK Rugby Club Directory Team
hello@anoziva.com

Expected conversion: 10–15% of clubs will add a link. 620 clubs × 12% = ~75 new backlinks from rugby club domains.

Month 4–8 · AI & Natural Links

AI-Assisted & Natural Link Acquisition

AI-Assisted Methods

  • AI content creation: Use AI to write county-specific "Rugby Clubs in [County]" pages that rank for local terms and attract natural links from clubs and local sites.
  • AI outreach personalisation: Generate personalised outreach emails for each club referencing their specific location, founding year and team structure.
  • AI PR angles: Identify newsworthy data stories from the directory (e.g. oldest clubs, fastest-growing regions) for pitching to rugby journalists.
  • Schema markup: AI-generated schema for every club page improves rich snippets and increases click-through from search results.

Natural Link Methods

  • Forum participation: Answer questions on r/rugbyunion, Total Rugby League Forum, WalesOnline rugby threads — cite directory where relevant.
  • Resource pages: Contact local council sport pages and ask to be listed as a rugby club finder resource.
  • Testimonials: Offer clubs featured testimonials on their kit/equipment suppliers' sites in exchange for a mention.
  • Wikipedia citations: Add the directory as a citation on rugby-related Wikipedia articles about clubs or regions.
  • Podcast appearances: Offer directory data/insights to rugby podcasts in exchange for links in show notes.

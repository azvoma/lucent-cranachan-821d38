About the Club

About Isle of Wight RFC

Island club with a welcoming atmosphere for all rugby fans on the Isle of Wight.

Isle of Wight RFC has been a proud part of the rugby union community in Newport, Isle of Wight since 1924. The club welcomes players of all ages and abilities and is committed to developing rugby from grassroots to senior level.

Our Story

History & Heritage

Isle of Wight RFC was founded in 1924, giving the club over 101 years of history in Newport, Isle of Wight. Over the decades the club has grown from humble beginnings into a thriving community organisation.

If you are looking for a rugby union club in Newport, Isle of Wight RFC could be the perfect fit. With a welcoming community, dedicated coaching staff and teams at multiple levels, there is something for every player.
